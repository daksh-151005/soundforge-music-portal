# 🎵 SoundForge — Translucent Glass Music Producer & Artist Platform

A streaming and management platform tailored for independent songwriters and music producers, designed with **interactive floating musical note particles**, **frosted translucent glass panels**, and **multiple switchable aesthetic themes**.

---

## ✨ What's New

### 🎼 1. Floating Musical Note Particles (Interactive Canvas)
- Ambient musical notes (`♪`, `♫`, `♬`, `♩`, `𝄞`, `𝄢`, `✦`) continuously drift across the empty space behind all panels.
- **Mouse Interaction**: As you move your mouse cursor across the screen, the musical notes dynamically react, gently swirling and moving away from your cursor with organic fluid inertia.
- **Live Theme Synchronization**: The color, glow aura, and visual characteristics of the musical notes change instantly with the active theme.

### 🪟 2. Translucent Frosted Glass Panels
- All cards, navigation headers, audio players, and modals now feature genuine **acrylic frosted glassmorphism** (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(170%)`).
- The floating musical notes in the background can now be subtly seen gliding and refracting behind the translucent cards.
- Specular edge highlights and subtle theme borders provide a modern, depth-filled aesthetic.

### 🎨 3. Multi-Theme Switcher
Switch between 5 curated visual themes on the fly from the **Theme Switcher** button in the navigation bar or Gateway Portal:

| Theme | Aesthetic & Vibe | Accents & Particle Palette |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔴 Crimson Onyx** *(Original)* | Pitch black & electric red | Crimson `#dc2626`, Ruby `#ef4444`, Blood red |
| **🟣 Neon Violet** | Cyberpunk late-night studio | Electric Violet `#8b5cf6`, Cyan `#06b6d4`, Magenta |
| **🟢 Emerald Noir** | Organic lo-fi & acoustic | Pine black, Emerald `#10b981`, Mint `#34d399` |
| **🟡 Vintage Vinyl** | Warm analog brass & wood | Espresso noir, Warm Amber `#f59e0b`, Gold `#fbbf24` |
| **🔵 Sapphire Abyss** | Deep oceanic chillout | Midnight navy, Electric Sapphire `#2563eb`, Sky Ice |

*The audio spectrum visualizer, seekbar, flame badges, glowing drop-shadows, and background notes all adapt simultaneously.*

### 🚪 4. Gateway Entrance & Private Creator Access
- **Gateway Portal**: Appears on entry with two doorway options:
  - **Viewer Lounge**: One-click guest entry or fan sign-in (with 1-click test shortcut).
  - **Artist Studio**: Clean, private access terminal with **zero credentials displayed**.
